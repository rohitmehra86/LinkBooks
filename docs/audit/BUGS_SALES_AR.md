# Sales / Accounts Receivable — bug audit

- **Scope:** `src/routes/_authenticated/sales/*`, `src/lib/sales/*`, `src/components/sales/*`, tax/rounding (`src/lib/tax/tax-math.ts`, `src/lib/purchases/line-math.ts`), posting (`src/lib/accounting/posting-engine.ts`, `post_journal` / `reverse_journal` RPCs), aged receivables (`src/lib/accounting/accounting.functions.ts`), recurring generation, customer refunds.
- **Branch:** `cto-master-programme` at `c4b0cc8`. Read-only audit.
- **Method:** I read the code and traced each flow end to end (UI → server function → `post_journal` → document status and amount updates). I ran SELECT-only checks on Supabase project `ipxflpzfcqwtenodbxwc` to confirm the live RLS policies and triggers and to look for data drift.
- **Note:** `src/lib/create-new/money-documents.functions.ts` had uncommitted edits from another session during this audit. Refund findings refer to the committed (`HEAD`) version.

Severity scale:
- **Critical:** wrong ledger, money posted without its documents, or a permission bypass.
- **High:** an accountant gets wrong numbers or is blocked from a core task.
- **Medium:** an incorrect or misleading result that has a workaround.
- **Low:** polish.

| AR-01 | Critical | `src/lib/sales/sales.functions.ts:804-906` | `recordReceipt` runs in this order: insert receipt → post DR Bank / CR AR journal → **then** validate each allocation (customer, currency, status, outstanding) in a loop. If any allocation fails, the receipt and journal are already committed, the error is thrown, and earlier allocations stay applied. Nothing runs in a DB transaction. | You get a "Allocation exceeds invoice outstanding balance" toast, but the bank and AR have already moved. Trying again posts a **second** receipt and journal, so cash is overstated. | Move the whole receipt into one `SECURITY DEFINER` RPC (`record_customer_receipt`) that validates every allocation first, then inserts the receipt, allocations and journal, and updates invoices in one transaction. At minimum, validate all allocations before inserting. |
| AR-02 | Critical | `supabase/migrations/20261019180000_sales_banking_contacts.sql:421-427` (live DB confirmed: no triggers on `sales_*`) | The `sales_invoices` UPDATE policy lets anyone with `sales.draft` (the **staff** role) update any column. ## Stubs, dummy and missing UI

- **Invoices**
  - No email, no "Mark as sent", no "Copy invoice", no attachments.
  - No delete for drafts.
  - No payment history panel.
  - Print/PDF is a browser print of the app page.
- **Receipts:** no detail page (`receipts.$id` does not exist), no void, no "allocate unallocated balance later", no batch receipts.
- **Credit notes:** no edit for drafts, no void, no "Credit from invoice", no tax entry, one line only, no refund button.
- **Quotes:** `saveQuoteDraft` supports editing, but there is **no edit route**. No email, no expiry automation.
- **Recurring:** create, pause and "Generate now" only. No edit, delete, tax, multi-line or end date.
- **Statements:** activity only (no opening balance), print only, no email, no batch statements.
- **Customers page:** name, email and terms only.

## Build next (prioritised)

1. **Transactional AR RPCs** for receipt, allocation, credit allocation, authorise and void (fixes AR-01, 03, 04, 11, 12, 15, 16), plus RLS/trigger lockdown of posted documents (AR-02). Without this the ledger cannot be trusted.
2. **Correction tools:** void receipt, unallocate payment or credit, void credit note, delete draft, void with a reversal date (AR-05, 21, 22).
3. **Proper credit notes:** tax, multiple lines, "credit from invoice", numbering, refund from credit or overpayment (AR-07, 14, 30).
4. **Correct AR reports:** aged receivables as at a date, including unapplied cash and credit, statements with opening balance and currency, SQL totals with no 1000-row cap (AR-08, 09, 26).
5. **Tax codes on sales lines,** with percentage entry and VAT-return mapping (AR-19, 18, 20).
6. **Invoice document and delivery:** branded PDF, email send with sent/viewed log, payment reminders, statement emails, attachments.
7. **Multi-currency invoicing:** FX rate capture, foreign display, realised and unrealised FX (AR-10).
8. **List usability:** search, filters, sort, pagination, bulk authorise/email/print, "Copy invoice", open-invoices-by-customer (AR-06, 27).
9. **Customer master:** addresses, credit limit with a warning on invoice, default tax/account, on-hold.
10. **Receivables extras:** batch deposits and receipts, bad-debt write-off (DR bad debt / CR AR with VAT relief), deposits and prepayments, early-payment discounts, late fees, full recurring templates (AR-28, 29), online payment links.
